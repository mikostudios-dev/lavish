# Identity review · current Lavish website · 20 October 2026

Reviewed visually and structurally in the iMac's Chrome browser, using the actual pages, before implementation:
- https://www.lavisheventrentals.com/
- https://www.lavisheventrentals.com/seating/
- https://www.lavisheventrentals.com/seating/abundant-chair/
- https://www.lavisheventrentals.com/about-us/
- https://www.lavisheventrentals.com/photo-gallery/

## What makes the site Lavish
1. A distinctive left-hand column with yellow/gray logo, right-aligned category navigation, then gallery/blog/contact. The content lives to its right.
2. A white canvas and gray typography. Pale yellow is a precise accent: logo, active links and hairline separators, rather than a dominant surface.
3. The current home is led by the sage sofa/ivory chair room photograph. The introduction sits below it, with a right-aligned heading and yellow horizontal rule.
4. Seating is a direct catalog of isolated furniture on white, four columns and concise product names. Subcategories are central to navigation.
5. The Abundant Chair page prioritizes the large object photo, alternate views, descriptive text and dimensions. Existing calendar/cost widgets were observed but are deliberately not copied into this no-price quote mockup.
6. About uses a furniture montage and a compact factual company story. Gallery is a large single-photo viewer with thumbnails and visible event/design credits.

## Current redesign
Preserve the lateral category architecture, original logo, white/gray/yellow palette, familiar hero photograph, product-on-white presentation, actual categories, Miami/Orlando positioning and attributed events. Modernize using the existing local Proxima font, discreet type, generous consistent spacing, a slim fixed sidebar, responsive mobile menu, user-selected room settings, useful catalog filters, clear local quote action and accessible dialogs. Keep image boundaries clean and photography full contrast.

The cinematic pinned scenes, dark moving gallery, perspective rotations and large scroll spacers from the rejected revision are removed. Native flow and at most 12px image drift support browsing. Pause and system reduced motion remove that drift. No new services, claims, prices or live-client modifications. Only mockups/v4 is edited.
