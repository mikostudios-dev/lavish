BORN IN MIAMI

Different backgrounds.
A shared point of view.

Lavish was founded in Miami by a team with experience in corporate event production, marketing and law, brought together by an appreciation for modern design.

Those different perspectives shaped a collection of event furniture and accessories that brings together modern classics, emerging trends and custom-designed pieces.

Today, Lavish furnishes weddings, corporate events, trade shows, private celebrations and photo and video shoots across South and Central Florida.
