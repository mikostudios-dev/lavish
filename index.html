Where do you deliver?+

We deliver event rental furniture across South and Central Florida from our Miami and Orlando locations. Contact Lavish to confirm delivery to your venue.

Can you create something custom?+

Yes. Our inventory is always evolving, and we can modify, brand or custom-build rental pieces. Tell us what you have in mind so we can explore the options.

Can I pick up my rental items?+

Yes. Please contact Lavish to coordinate pickup and drop-off from the warehouse.

Do you offer longer rentals?+

Yes. Long-term and multi-day rentals are available. Contact us to discuss the needs of your event.
